## Role Overview

**Title:** Technical Architect  
**Duration:** March 2021 → July 2025 (4y 5m)  
**Company:** Magma S.R.L.  
**Office:** Naples, Italy  
**Key Stakeholders / Clients:**  
- Walgreens Boots Alliance (US)

**Main Responsibilities**  
- Architectural design  
- Modernization initiatives  
- Performance engineering  
- FinOps practices  
- Scrum coordination  
- R&D activities  


As a Technical Architect at Magma, I contributed to the Retail Pharmacy Integration (RxI) platform for Walgreens Boots Alliance.  
This cloud-native system was fully hosted on Microsoft **Azure** and deployed across nearly **10,000 US pharmacies and drugstores**.  
My responsibilities included architectural design, modernization initiatives, performance optimisation, and day-to-day collaboration with both Italian and US engineering teams.

### Key Responsibilities & Achievements
- Designed and evolved cloud-native architectures leveraging Azure services such as Functions, AKS, Event Hub, Service Bus, API Management, and Cosmos DB.  
- Contributed to the successful chain-wide rollout of the new RxI platform across **~10,000 pharmacies**.  
- Led the migration of all microservices to **Java 11**, standardising base container images, CI/CD pipelines, and shared internal libraries across the platform.  
- Achieved a **≈70% reduction in API and UI response times** through targeted performance improvements and workload optimisations.  
- Optimised database models, indexing strategies and query patterns, reducing operational costs by **≈20%**.  
- Provided architectural guidance, defined technology standards, and supported development teams through code reviews and best-practice adoption.  
- Collaborated closely with US-based teams for alignment on architecture decisions, incident response, and the design of new features.  
- Managed a small engineering team focused on developing reusable internal frameworks (“common-utilities”), including **Scrum coordination** and onboarding of new engineers.  
- Conducted Proofs of Concept (PoCs) and Proofs of Technology (PoTs) for strategic platform enhancements and long-term **R&D** initiatives.
